# Real user questions: STW Guide

Written the way my user types them. User: MBBS intern posted at a Primary Health Centre near Kanchipuram who sees about 80 OPD patients a day.

These 10 questions become my first evaluation set in Week 3. Question 6 is not answerable from my documents: the product must say "I don't know".

| # | Question | Type | Where the answer is (document, page) | More than one page? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What are the red flags in the ICMR STW for acute diarrhoea in children? | Simple | STW for Management of Acute Diarrhea — page to verify | No |
| 2 | What first-line management does the STW give for community-acquired pneumonia in adults? | Simple | STW for Management of Acute Respiratory Infection in Adults — page to verify | No |
| 3 | When does the hypertension STW advise referral to a higher centre? | Simple | STW: Hypertension in Adults — page to verify | No |
| 4 | A 45-year-old man at the PHC has fever for 5 days, low platelets and abdominal pain. Which STW applies, what are the warning signs and when must I refer him? | Multi-step (several pages) | Adult-appropriate dengue/fever STW — source and pages to verify | Yes |
| 5 | Compare the referral criteria in the STWs for dengue and for malaria. | Comparison | Dengue STW and Malaria STW — both sources and pages to verify | Yes |
| 6 | Is the district hospital ICU bed free tonight? | Unanswerable | None — the information is not available in the documents. The system must say "I don't know". | No |
| 7 | What are the danger signs in a child with pneumonia, and when should the child be referred? | Multi-step | ICMR STW for childhood pneumonia/acute respiratory infection — source and pages to verify | Yes |
| 8 | What investigations are recommended in the STW for suspected dengue? | Simple | ICMR STW for Dengue Fever — page to verify | No |
